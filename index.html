About

I'm a Business Analytics and Information Systems major at the University of South Florida's Muma College of Business, concentrating in cybersecurity and graduating in May 2028.

Since May 2025 I've worked for myself building custom AI chat agents for small business websites — tuning conversational flows, wiring up integrations, and cutting down the hours owners spend answering the same questions by hand. Most of what I build starts as something I wanted to exist: a betting model, a newsletter that writes itself, a widget that handles a restaurant's front desk.

Outside of that I sit on my fraternity's finance committee managing a $70K semester budget, and I serve monthly at Trinity Cafe here in Tampa.
